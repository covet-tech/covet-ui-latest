The fine print

The weekly giveaway,
rules and all.

One real prize, every week, free to enter. No purchase is necessary and a purchase does not improve any single entry's chance of winning.

Effective August 2026 · Questions? The live support button in the footer reaches a real human.

The basics

Sponsor: SD1 Entertainment LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company doing business as Covet, 30 N Gould St, Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801.

Covet runs one giveaway per week. The prize, its approximate market value, and the drawing time are shown on the home page. Drawings happen Sunday midnight ET. If we switch between weekly and monthly giveaways, the change starts with the next giveaway and is announced on the home page; a giveaway already running keeps its announced drawing time. The winner is selected by random.org, an independent randomness provider, using their signed drawing service, and every entry has an equal chance.

How to enter

  • Free daily entries: 5 entries every day, just for visiting and claiming them. This is the free entry route and it requires no purchase, ever.
  • Opening boxes: every $1 opened adds 1 entry.

Eligibility

Open to residents of the United States, Puerto Rico included, who are 18 or older (19+ in Alabama and Nebraska), excluding Washington, Idaho, Hawaii, Kentucky, and South Carolina. Employees of Covet and household members are not eligible. One account per person; entries from duplicate accounts are removed.

Winning

The winner is notified by email within 24 hours of the drawing and has 7 days to confirm. If unconfirmed after 7 days, a new winner is drawn the same way. Winning entries are verified before the prize ships: if the winning entry turns out to be invalid, for example it came from a duplicate account, that entry is removed and the drawing is rerun the same way with the remaining valid entries. The prize ships free and insured. Winners may be announced by first name and last initial; we ask permission before using anything more.

The fine print

Prizes have no cash alternative unless the listed prize becomes unavailable, in which case the winner may choose an equivalent item or Covet credit at the prize's listed value. Giveaways are void where prohibited. These giveaways are not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with TikTok or Instagram.
