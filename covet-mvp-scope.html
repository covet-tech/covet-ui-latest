Covet, the MVP

What it is, and what came out · September 2026 · goes with the prototype

Covet is a mystery box storefront for women. Customers buy a box with published, verifiable odds, reveal a real luxury item, and either ship it free or sell it back instantly for site credit. Around that core: free daily boxes, a weekly giveaway, creator referral codes, and an operator panel that runs the whole thing. The prototype shows every screen of it working; this page is the map of what's in the build and what deliberately isn't.

What came out

  • iOS app (later, with you, at a premium, once there's revenue)
  • The Boutique storefront
  • Bank transfers and any payout rail. Launch is credit only
  • Crypto payments
  • Microservices. One deployable app, one Postgres database, one background worker
  • Creator claim approvals and in-product tax forms. Creator earnings just auto-credit; tax paperwork is handled manually at my volume
  • External penetration testing (I bring my own vendor; your internal testing stays, plus cooperation on fix cycles)
  • Discovery as a paid phase. The prototype and spec are the backlog

What the MVP is

  • Accounts: email plus code verification, Google sign in, password reset
  • KYC (Didit), one time, triggered at first free box claim, first shipment, or first gift card trade
  • Payment integration: the processor I designate, hosted checkout plus webhooks, wallet funding and pay the difference checkout
  • Wallet ledger with distinct credit classes (deposits, earned credit, boxes-only locked credit); exit rules per the build spec
  • Provably fair reveals via the random.org API connection: signed seeds, the exact spec and working verifier are on the prototype's fairness page
  • Covet boxes with published odds, four reveal styles
  • Customer wishlist boxes with automatic pricing and capped odds
  • Shelf: ship free or instant buyback on every pull
  • Free daily boxes with claim caps and streaks
  • Weekly giveaway: 5 free entries daily, 1 entry per dollar, random.org draw
  • Creator codes with automatic earnings credit
  • Admin: box builder with supplier catalog sync over the Brands Gateway and FragranceX API connections, plus custom manual items, odds editing with auto balance, buyback dials, fulfillment queue with stock-out flow, low stock panel with box on/off, users with flags and per user shelves, transaction lookup, creators view with code editing, stats, master switches (maintenance, deposits, gift cards, giveaway, daily program)
  • Transactional email, event tracking, 404, everything else demonstrated in the prototype
